Rules

ColourBrackets are white on dark and near-black on light. The eye and waveform are the brand cyan; on white they darken to #0A7F96 to keep contrast.

SpaceKeep clear space equal to one bracket arm (28 of 96 units) on every side. Wordmark sits to the right of the mark, vertically centred, with the Devanagari line beneath it.

NeverRotate, outline, add a glow or shadow, redraw the waveform with more bars, or put the cyan eye on yellow, green or orange.

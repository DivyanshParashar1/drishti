Notes for engineering

  • No blur anywhere. Scrims are flat alpha fills; the listening glow is an edge gradient or a 3 dp stroke, not a shadow.
  • Every filled tag uses black text, so alert colours are never the only carrier: words (NEAR, FAR), shape (solid vs dashed) and position back them up.
  • The cane line, the consent badge and the OFFLINE badge are the three places where the UI states a safety or privacy fact; each has its own treatment.
